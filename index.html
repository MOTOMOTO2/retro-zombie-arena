      HOW TO PLAY

        EARN POINTS by hitting and killing the dead. Spend them on doors, wall guns, the mystery box and perk machines.

        HOLD F at a boarded window to nail the planks back on.

        EVERY CHAPTER HAS A WAY OUT. Restore the power, find the three parts, build what Voss asks for at the workbench — then survive what comes for you, and escape. The yellow-green marker shows where to go next.

        FIND THE GENERATOR. The map is different every night, but the power is always as far from the start as it can be. No power, no perks, no Pack-a-Punch.

        MYSTERY BOX rolls a random gun — sometimes a legendary. PACK-A-PUNCH takes a gun three tiers deep.

        BOSSES telegraph every move. Jump over a slam, sidestep a charge — a boss that hits a wall is stunned.

        EVERY FIFTH ROUND the hellhounds come instead. They are fast. The last one always drops max ammo.

        KEEP MOVING. Nothing in here out-runs a trained horde except a player in a circle.
